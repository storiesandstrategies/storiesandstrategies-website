More than a Third of New Podcasts are AI-Generated

Pod Slop

According to new data from Podcast Index, more than a third of all new podcasts being created right now are AI-generated, and one company alone, Inception Point AI, is responsible for nearly a quarter of all new podcast output.

Jeanine Wright, the CEO of Inception Point AI told the Hollywood Reporter, “We believe that in the near future half the people on the planet will be AI, and we are the company that’s bringing those people to life.”

Podcasting became one of the most powerful mediums on the planet precisely because it is deeply, irreducibly human, a real person’s voice, perspective, and personality coming through your earbuds like a conversation with a friend. The whole value proposition of this medium is intimacy, and you cannot manufacture intimacy with a language model churning out five-minute episodes about the definition of the word ‘literally.’ AI-generated slop is not the future of podcasting; it’s noise that the best creators in this space will rise above, but we should all be paying attention to how fast that noise is growing.

Why Brands Are Looking Beyond the Big Shows

The podcast industry is shifting its focus from mega-shows to mid-sized creators, and that's good news for independents. Executives at last month's NAB Show made the case that shows with around 100,000 downloads can be highly attractive to advertisers. Especially direct-response brands that care more about engaged, targeted listeners than raw scale.

New tools like programmatic buying and improved analytics are making it easier for brands to find the right audiences, while YouTube is emerging as a major discovery engine, helping creators reach listeners well beyond their home markets.

AI is also playing a growing role in streamlining production and matching brands with the right shows more efficiently.

What Kind of Content Works Best in AI Search

Vince Nero at Buzzstream looked into this.

Here are his top findings and keep in mind prompt type has substantial impact on outputs:

  • No single site dominates AI citation. Reddit leads at just 3.49%

  • Prompt type drives citations more than domain

  • Blog/content makes up 53.46% of all citations, far ahead of news (14.09%) and social (8.71%)

  • Owned content appears in ~40% of brand awareness queries.

  • Earned media accounts for ~80% of citations when brand-agnostic queries are excluded

  • ChatGPT is more controllable, with 68.9% of citations coming from owned content

  • Social citations skew toward the bottom of the funnel, with 18% of evaluative queries vs. ~5% for informational queries

  • Reddit drives 39.87% of social citations

Cleveland.com's AI Avatar Disaster Has a Simple Lesson for Podcasters

Cleveland.com replaced its podcast hosts with AI-generated cartoon avatars to promote its shows on social media, using the hosts’ real voices but cheerful animated likenesses instead of, well, the actual people.

The backlash was swift and universal.

But the real mistake wasn’t using AI, it’s that a local news audience built on trust in real journalists is about the worst possible fit for AI slop promos.

Know your listeners: a tech or AI podcast could pull this off without a second thought, but if your audience follows you for human credibility, sending a cartoon in your place is a fast way to tell them you don’t get them at all.
